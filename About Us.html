Welcome to Voice of wits

How Voice of Wits Was Created

Voice of Wits was born from a simple idea: students needed a platform that truly belonged to them. Created by Wits students, for Wits students, the station grew out of late-night conversations, shared frustrations, and a collective desire to hear more authentic voices on campus.

What started as a small passion project quickly became a student-led movement. A group of dedicated Witsies came together with microphones, laptops, and big dreams — determined to build a space where real campus life could be heard, celebrated, and shared.

Today, Voice of Wits continues to grow as a student-driven platform that reflects the energy, diversity, and spirit of the University of the Witwatersrand.

What You’ll Hear on Voice of Wits

At Voice of Wits, we believe radio should do more than just play songs. We carefully curate a blend of the latest hits, timeless classics, and hidden gems across genres so there’s always something that feels like it was made for you.

Talented presenters host engaging talk shows, lively interviews, and thought-provoking discussions that explore everything from everyday life to the bigger questions that make us human.

From morning drive-time energy to late-night chill sessions, our schedule is designed to match the rhythm of your day. Expect fresh playlists, exclusive artist features, community stories, and moments that make you smile, reflect, or simply turn the volume up.

We’re more than just a station – we’re a companion that understands the power of a great song and a good conversation.

A Space for Students

Voice of Wits is more than a radio station — it’s a student space. Here, Witsies talk openly about campus life, share their experiences, and connect with one another in real and meaningful ways.

We keep students motivated through uplifting conversations, study tips, mental health check-ins, and stories that remind you that you’re not alone in the journey. Whether you’re grinding for exams or navigating first-year life, we’re here to keep your spirit high.

Stay in the loop with the latest University events, campus updates, important announcements, and changes happening around Wits. From residence news and society events to academic deadlines and student opportunities — if it matters to Wits students, you’ll hear about it here.

This is your station. Your voice. Your campus.

No matter where you are or what you’re doing, we’re right here with you, ready to fill your world with sound and substance.
