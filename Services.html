What We Offer

Voice of Wits is more than just a radio station. We provide a range of services designed to inform, entertain, support, and connect the Wits community.

Music & Entertainment

  • Curated playlists featuring the latest hits, local music, classics, and hidden gems
  • Live DJ sets and request shows
  • Artist interviews and exclusive performances
  • Genre-specific shows including Amapiano, Afrobeats, Hip-Hop, Jazz, and more

Campus News & Information

  • Daily and weekly campus news bulletins
  • Updates on university events, deadlines, and important announcements
  • Coverage of campus changes and new developments
  • Residence and student society news

Talk Shows & Student Conversations

  • Student-led talk shows about real campus life
  • Discussions and debates on current issues
  • Interviews with lecturers, student leaders, and special guests
  • Open platforms for students to share their voices and stories

Academic Support & Motivation

  • Study tips and exam preparation advice
  • Motivational content during high-pressure academic periods
  • Conversations that remind students they are not alone
  • Mental health and wellbeing check-ins

Events & Live Coverage

  • Live broadcasts from major campus events
  • Coverage of sports, cultural days, and festivals
  • Promotion of society events and student initiatives
  • Special programming during Orientation Week and key university periods

Skills Development

  • Training in presenting, producing, and radio production
  • Hands-on media and journalism experience
  • Opportunities to build a professional portfolio
  • A platform for Communication and Media students to grow

Digital & On-Demand Content

  • Live streaming online
  • Podcast versions of popular shows
  • Social media highlights and behind-the-scenes content
  • On-demand access to past episodes

Community & Student Engagement

  • A space for student stories and real experiences
  • Shout-outs, dedications, and personal messages
  • Competitions and giveaways
  • Opportunities for students to host their own shows

Voice of Wits – serving the campus, amplifying student voices, and keeping Witsies connected.
